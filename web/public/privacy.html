Privacy

Updated 14 September 2026.

Planner

Plans, watchlists, major and credit-band preferences are stored in this browser. Registration Sniper does not create a personal account. The Cloudflare service handles public course/CRN requests and shares public catalog and seat observations across visitors. Cloudflare processes network requests to deliver the website. Browser storage does not mean that no network data leaves the device.

Notifications

Optional Telegram delivery sends messages directly from the browser to Telegram. The personal bot token is kept in this tab’s session storage and is not sent to the Registration Sniper server. Telegram receives the message and delivery metadata. Disconnect removes the saved token; browser session recovery may restore tab storage. Sound and desktop alerts operate on the local device.

Extension

The side panel reads prepared CRNs, term and monitor observations from the connected planner tab. It inspects registration-page fields, fills recognized CRN inputs and verifies their values. Optional Aimbot prepare mode can follow a recognized Add/Drop link and submit a semester choice, then fill once after a new seat opening. Final registration submission is manual. The extension does not read or store university passwords.

Connection settings are kept in chrome.storage.session without browser synchronization. Optional local Python mode uses a local bridge key sent to 127.0.0.1. No analytics or advertising SDK is included, and no university credentials are sent to Registration Sniper’s Cloudflare service.

Control

Pause seat monitoring, switch off Aimbot and disconnect Telegram to stop their respective actions. Closing the side panel disarms Aimbot. Clear this website’s browser storage to remove local plans and preferences; export a plan first if needed. Removing the extension removes its stored connection settings.
